i. Our story

Every dent is a story

Long before steel and plastic, every home had its vessels: a brass kindi by the door, a copper pot for drinking water, a clay jar keeping things cool in the corner. They were made by hand, used every day and handed down through generations.

Chimizh looks for these vessels in old homes, trading families and forgotten storerooms. We clean them gently without erasing their age, and find them homes where they will be noticed again.

iii. Materials

What old vessels are made of

BrassBrass

Brass, copper and zinc together, was the gold of the household: polished for festivals and passed down at weddings.

CopperCopper

Copper was trusted to keep drinking water pure. It glows rose-gold, darkens with age, and was lined with tin for cooking.

Bell MetalBell Metal

Bell metal, a high-tin bronze, rings when struck. It was cast by hand into urulis, kindis and temple lamps.

TerracottaTerracotta

Terracotta, fired river clay, kept water cool through the summer. It was carved and stamped by village potters.
